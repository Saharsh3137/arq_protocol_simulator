Why does Selective Repeat require Ws + Wr ≤ 2m?

If all ACKs in a window are lost in transit, the sender will time out and retransmit with the same sequence numbers. If $W_s + W_r > 2^m$, the sender's retransmitted sequence numbers will overlap with the receiver's next expected window, causing the receiver to mistake duplicate retransmissions for brand new data!
